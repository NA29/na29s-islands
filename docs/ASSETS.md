# Bring your own wallpaper

The distributed background is a two-stop gradient: `#342d40` → `#162a37`, at 145°.
No wallpaper art, audio, fonts, caches, or activity history are distributed here.

Put an image you own or have permission to use in `~/Pictures/Wallpapers`, then
choose it in **Settings → Wallpaper**. The picker copies a display-sized image
into your local Herdr config and creates local thumbnails. Those files belong
to your installation, not this showcase repo. Herdr's built-in notification
sounds are used unless you choose your own.

The wallpaper in the screenshots is Hatsune Miku fan art picked by the author;
it is not included and not licensed by this repo.

Screenshots are crops of actual Herdr frames. Their colors and opacity values
reflect the live setup at capture time; the installable defaults come from the
clean config snapshot. See [the capture checklist](SCREENSHOTS.md).
